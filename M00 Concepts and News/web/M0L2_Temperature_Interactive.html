Temperature Scaling

Control the randomness and creativity of LLM outputs by scaling the probability distribution

Mathematical Foundation

Temperature-Scaled Softmax

$$P(y_i) = \frac{e^{z_i / T}}{\sum_{j=1}^{n} e^{z_j / T}}$$

Where $T$ is the temperature parameter ($T > 0$)

Effect of Temperature

  • T → 0: Deterministic (argmax)
  • T = 1: Standard softmax
  • T > 1: Flatter distribution (more random)
  • T → ∞: Uniform distribution

Key Takeaways

✓ Temperature scales logits before softmax

✓ Low T → peaked distribution (deterministic)

✓ High T → flat distribution (random)

✓ Gemini default: T=1.0, range [0.0, 2.0]

✓ Entropy increases monotonically with T

✓ Choose T based on task requirements
